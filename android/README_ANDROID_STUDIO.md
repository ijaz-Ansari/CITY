# City Con Fee Management System - Native Android Project

This is the complete Native Android Studio project wrapper for **City Con & Allied Health Sciences Nowshera Virkan Fee Management System**.

## 🚀 Quick Start: Building the APK in Android Studio

1. **Open in Android Studio**:
   - Launch **Android Studio**.
   - Click **Open** and select this `android` folder.
   - Wait for Gradle sync to complete (auto-downloads SDK 34 dependencies).

2. **Generate APK (Debug / Release)**:
   - Click **Build** in the top menu.
   - Select **Build Bundle(s) / APK(s)** ➔ **Build APK(s)**.
   - Once completed, click **locate** in the popup notification to get your `.apk` file!
   - Transfer `app-debug.apk` to any Android mobile phone via WhatsApp/USB/Google Drive and tap to install.

3. **Direct Run on Android Device via USB**:
   - Enable **Developer Options** & **USB Debugging** on your Android phone.
   - Connect phone via USB cable.
   - Click the green **▶ Run** button in Android Studio.
   - The app installs and opens directly on your phone!

## 📱 Features of the Android App

- **Full-Screen Native Experience**: Runs without the browser URL bar, just like WhatsApp or banking apps.
- **Direct Receipt & Voucher Printing**: Integrated with Android `PrintManager` for printing Challan copies and Fee Slips directly to Wi-Fi/Bluetooth printers or saving as PDF on the device.
- **Offline Storage & Caching**: Students, ledger transactions, and daily summaries are stored in HTML5 IndexedDB / localStorage.
- **Hardware Back Button Handling**: Navigates smoothly between screens without unexpectedly exiting the app.
- **Campus Camera & File Chooser**: Supports taking student admission photos or picking images from the phone gallery.
